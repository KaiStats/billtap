Files are the latin-subset woff2 builds served by Google Fonts, retrieved
from fonts.gstatic.com. Inter, Inter Tight and JetBrains Mono are variable
fonts covering the weight ranges declared in src/index.css.

Under OFL 1.1 these files may be redistributed provided they are not sold
on their own, and the Reserved Font Names are not used on modified versions.
Neither applies here: they are served unmodified as part of the application.
